Latent Sky

HOW IT IS BUILT · AND WHAT HAS BEEN PROVEN

The concept had two boxes. The architecture has three — and the middle one is what makes weekends survivable.

The expensive, one-shot GPU stage produces a small archive and stops. Every decision about colour, resolution and framing then becomes a free laptop re-run. The browser gets a folder of files.

And the hero moment is no longer a plan: the reveal already runs on real typhoon data, on localhost, verified in real Chrome.

The build, end to end

Each stage hands the next a file. Only the first costs money, and it runs once.

What measurement overturned

Five things the concept assumed, corrected by downloading the models and reading their source — before they could cost a weekend.

A reveal on radar reflectivity
The reveal is 10 m wind speed
The coarse model produces no reflectivity at all — there is no "before" to show. Reflectivity becomes its own panel: a field the coarse model cannot make.
"Pick a hero region"
Taiwan — not a choice
Exactly one public kilometre-scale CorrDiff checkpoint exists, and Taiwan is baked into its weights. Europe returns HTTP 401; CONUS is gated.
FourCastNet-3 as the global model
SFNO — half the GPU
FCN3 wants 80 GB and has a documented unresolved OOM on 46 GB cards. SFNO runs on the 40 GB class and is the model in NVIDIA's validated example.
Output "446×446", docs say 3 km
448×448 · 2.0684 km — measured
Read from the actual XLAT/XLONG tensors. The concept's ~2 km was right and earth2studio's own example page is wrong.
Paint the output straight onto the globe
A regrid stage is mandatory
The grid is rotated: longitude drifts up to 37 km down one column. Painted as-is, Taiwan's weather would sit 9% of the island away from where it belongs.
"First AI weather globe"
Claim the architecture, not novelty
NVIDIA and DeepMind ship AI weather visuals — but every interactive Earth-2 visual NVIDIA ships needs a GPU at view time. Ours is a folder of files. That claim survived every attempt to falsify it.

The two numbers the project rests on

Both measured from real data inside NVIDIA's freely downloadable package — no GPU, no credentials, reproducible by one script.

23.6 → 52.2 m/s
Peak 10 m wind over the same sea, same moment, during Typhoon Chanthu. The 25 km model does not render the eyewall blurrily — it cannot represent it at all. The coarse layer only ever reaches the lower half of the shared colour ramp; that asymmetry is the finding, rendered.
1,440 → 200,704 cells
Grid cells in, grid cells out: 36×40 at ~25 km becomes 448×448 at ~2.07 km — a 139× increase, every new cell generated by a diffusion model that has learned what fine-scale atmosphere looks like.

The bug that would have ruined the reveal

Probe 3 painted the globe one flat colour and sampled the rendered pixel at two camera heights. It should be identical. With Cesium's defaults, it is not.

The payload, measured

Every band below comes from encoding real fields at final geometry — typhoon-weighted, because that is what the hero run will look like. Real coastlines cost 2.3× the synthetic estimate on global layers, so the global cadence dropped to 12-hourly and the budget still clears.
